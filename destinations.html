Explore Destinations

Upstate New York offers a variety of destinations that cater to different interests. From natural attractions to museums and historic towns, there are plenty of places to discover.

Explore our selection of destinations and learn more about what makes each one unique and worth visiting.

Niagara Falls State Park

Niagara Falls State Park is known for its breathtaking waterfalls and beautiful views of the Niagara River.

Letchworth State Park

Letchworth State Park features deep gorges, scenic trails, and three major waterfalls along the Genesee River.

Watkins Glen State Park

Watkins Glen State Park is famous for its beautiful gorge, waterfalls, and stone bridges along its hiking trails.

Boldt Castle

Boldt Castle is a historic castle located on Heart Island in the Thousand Islands region of New York.

Corning Museum of Glass

The Corning Museum of Glass features glass art, exhibits, and live glassmaking demonstrations.
